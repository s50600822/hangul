전도사 EP.01
사나, 미연이랑 주먹다짐으로 평생 친구를 맺다

Guest Sana joins the host for a stunt-team day: stretching, punches, wire flying, and filming a sword-fight scene. Hanja (漢字) are given for Sino-Korean words; — means native Korean or loanword.

Setting: Stunt studio  •  Style: casual 해요체 / 반말 + on-screen captions  •  Genre: Variety / Action

01 Guessing the Guest from the Self-Diagnosis Form

만약에 몰랐다 하더라도 이 글씨체를 알고 있거든요.
Manyage mollatda hadeorado i geulssicheul algo itgeodeunyo.
"Even if I hadn’t known, I recognize this handwriting, you see."

05 Basic Punches, Choreography, and the Quiz

왜냐하면 주먹질을 한 번도 안 해봐서 그래요.
Waenyahamyeon jumeokjireul han beondo an haebwaseo geuraeyo.
"Because you’ve never thrown a punch even once."

주먹질—jumeokjilNounpunching (derogatory -질)
동작動作dongjakNounmovement; motion
시선視線siseonNoungaze

Grammar: -질 is the same derogatory suffix as 이간질 (see EP.00 vocabulary). 한 번도 안 -아/어 = “not even once.” 왜냐하면 ~ -아/어서 그래요 = explaining the reason. -는 도중에 = “in the middle of.” -(으)면 되다 = “you only need to.” The quiz forces answers mid-move: 무의식 입출력 테스트 (“unconscious I/O test”).

06 Wire Action and Flying

Waieoneun siljero jagiga jeompeureul hamyeon an dwaeyo. Jeompeuhaneun sinyeong yeongireul hasyeoya haeyo!

탕수육 투어
대한민국 탕수육 왕중왕전

A food vlogger tours seven tangsuyuk (sweet & sour pork) restaurants and rates each one. A great source of review vocabulary, hedged opinions, and spoken-Korean grammar. Hanja (漢字) are given for Sino-Korean words; — means native Korean or a loanword with no hanja.

Setting: Seoul, Jeju  •  Style: casual 해요체 narration with 반말 asides  •  Genre: Food review / rating

01 The Premise: Why a Tangsuyuk Tour?

빵 투어는 좀 흔하고, 돈가스 투어는 솔직히 돈가스 너무 많이 먹어봐서 탕수육 투어로 하기로 했어요.
Ppang tueoneun jom heunhago, donkkaseu tueoneun soljikhi donkkaseu neomu mani meogeobwaseo tangsuyuk tueoro hagiro haesseoyo.
"Bread tours are kind of common, and honestly I’ve eaten so much donkatsu that I decided on a tangsuyuk tour."

솔직히率直-soljikhiAdverbhonestly

부먹 / 찍먹—bumeok / jjingmeokSlang nounspour-sauce-on / dip-in-sauce eater

Grammar: -기로 하다 = “decide to” (하기로 했어요). -아/어 봐서 = “because I’ve tried (so much).” -(이)라는 곳 = “a place called…” (‘대가방’이라는 곳). 부먹/찍먹 come from 붓다 (pour) and 찍다 (dip) + 먹다 — the endless Korean debate.

02 대가방: Describing Texture and Taste

탕수육만 엄청 먹었는데도 약간 질리지 않는 그런 탕수육의 맛이었어요.
Tangsuyungman eomcheong meogeonneundedo yakgan jilliji anneun geureon tangsuyugui masieosseoyo.
"Even though I ate nothing but tangsuyuk, it was the kind of tangsuyuk you don’t get tired of."

Grammar: -는데도 = “even though/despite.” -지 않는 negates a modifier (질리지 않는 맛 = a taste that doesn’t tire you). -아/어 줬었어요 (추천해 줬었어요) is a past-perfect “had done for me.” -까지 가야 돼? = “do I have to go all the way to…?” Review tip: N에 충실하다 is the standard praise for a no-gimmick dish.

04 강동원: Hype, Counterfactuals, and Honest Disappointment

만약에 꿔바로우가 이 세상에 나오지 않았다면 이걸 먹고 너무 신세계여서 10점 줬을 것 같아.
Manyage kkwobarouga i sesange naoji anatdamyeon igeol meokgo neomu sinsegyeoseo yeol jeom jwosseul geot gata.
"If kkwobarou (guo bao rou) had never been invented, I’d have been blown away and probably given this a 10."
KoreanHanjaRomanizationPart of speechGloss

신세계新世界sinsegyeNouna whole new world (amazement)

Grammar: 만약에 ~ -지 않았다면 … -았/었을 것 같다 is a counterfactual: “if X hadn’t happened, I would have…” 기대를 너무 많이 해서 그런 것 같아요 = “I think it’s because I expected too much” (V-아/어서 그런 것 같다 for guessing a cause). -는 와중에 = “in the middle of.” -(으)ㄹ 때는 / 따졌을 때는 = “when you weigh it.” -ㄴ/는 게 아니다 in “맛이 없다는 거 아닙니다” softens criticism.

05 우리동네 정육점 & 옛날 중국집

이게 탕수육이라고 쳐도 되는가? 고기를 이렇게 아낌없이 주셔도 되는가?
Ige tangsuyugirago chyeodo doeneunga? Gogireul ireoke akkimeopsi jusyeodo doeneunga?
"Can I even call this tangsuyuk? Is it okay to be given this much meat so generously?"

Grammar: -는가? is a self-addressed, slightly literary question — used for dramatic musing (“is this allowed?”). -(이)라고 쳐도 되다 = “is it okay to count it as…” -ㄴ 것 치고는 = “considering that…” (1시간 지난 것 치고는 맛있는). -(으)ㄹ 것 같다 / -ㄹ 수 있을 것 같아 is the host’s constant hedge. -ㄴ/은 걸 알 것 같은 맛 = “a taste that makes you understand why.”

06 팔선 & 제주 감귤탕수육

기본이라고 말하기 미안할 정도로 맛있는 탕수육이었다.
Gibonirago malhagi mianhal jeongdoro masinneun tangsuyugieotda.
"It was so delicious that it felt wrong to call it ‘basic’."
KoreanHanjaRomanizationPart of speechGloss
기교技巧gigyoNountechnique; flashy skill

감귤柑橘gamgyulNountangerine (Jeju specialty)

Grammar: -(으)ㄹ 정도로 = “to the extent that.” -기 때문에 (비쌌기 때문에) = a formal reason. V-기는 V-다 (맛있기는 맛있더라고요) repeats a verb to concede a point — “it IS tasty, but…” -(으)ㄹ까 하는 생각 = “a thought of whether…” -더라고요 reports a fresh personal discovery. Cultural note: the host decides not to deduct stars for price — a common debate in Korean reviews (가성비 vs. 맛).

07 The Conclusion: Is a Tangsuyuk 맛집 Worth It?

차라리 짜장면 맛집이면 짜장면 맛집이지, 거기서 탕수육을 곁들이면 되는 거지. 굳이 탕수육 맛집을 가서 먹어야 되나?
Charari jjajangmyeon masjibimyeon jjajangmyeon masjibiji, geogiseo tangsuyugeul gyeoteurimyeon doeneun geoji. Guni tangsuyuk masjibeul gaseo meogeoya doena?
"If it’s a jjajangmyeon place, then it’s a jjajangmyeon place — you just add tangsuyuk on the side. Why go out of your way for a tangsuyuk place?"

08 Key Review Patterns

1. -(으)ㄹ 것 같다 — hedged opinion (맛있을 것 같아요).
2. -더라고요 — personal discovery (바삭하더라고요).
3. N에 충실하다 / N에 치중되어 있다 — focused on N.
4. V-기는 V-다 — concession (맛있기는 맛있다).
5. -(이)라고 쳐도 되다 / -ㄴ 것 치고는 — counting as / considering that.
6. 만약에 ~ -았/었다면 … -았/었을 것 같다 — counterfactual.
7. 굳이 / 차라리 — rhetorical contrast.

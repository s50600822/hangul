Korean Vlog · Intermediate · Travel & Food

하노이 음식 투어

A Korean couple returns to Vietnam and tries Hanoi specialties: bún chả, bún ốc, chả cá, nộm bò khô, and Muscovy duck. Clear food-review vocabulary and everyday couple talk. Hanja (漢字) given for Sino-Korean words; — means native Korean or loanword.

Setting: Hanoi, Vietnam  •  Style: casual 해요체 / 반말  •  Genre: Travel & food vlog

01 Returning to Vietnam

3개월 만에 저희가 베트남에 돌아왔습니다.
Sam-gaewol maene jeohuiga beteunam-e doraowatseumnida.
"After three months, we have returned to Vietnam."

주로主-juroAdverbmainly
여행旅行yeohaengNoun / 하다trip; travel
그립다—geuripdaㅂ-irregular adj.to miss; to long for
Grammar: 기간 + 만에 = “after [a period]” (3개월 만에). -부터 시작해서 = “starting from.” -(으)려고 해요 = “plan to.” -(으)ㄹ 거예요 = future. -(으)ㄹ게요 (먹어볼게요) offers or announces an action. The couple mixes formal 합쇼체 (돌아왔습니다) with soft 해요체.

07 Muscovy Duck and Final Thoughts

건강한 음식을 정말 맛있게 먹고 싶다면 아마 이렇게 먹으면 될 것 같아.
